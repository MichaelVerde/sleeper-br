Week 7 brought six matchups, two absolute massacres, one undefeated team refusing to die, and a collection of waiver decisions that suggested several managers should have their FAAB accounts frozen.

## The Undefeated Machine Keeps Rolling

### Tom 126.20, Griff 99.62

Seven weeks. Seven wins. Apparently the rest of the league was just participating in Tom’s extended preseason.

Griff had now lost three straight after starting 4–1 and could not even break 100 against the league’s only undefeated team. Meanwhile, Tom’s 965 FAAB investment was looking increasingly like a successful hostile takeover.

## The Week’s Biggest Public Execution

### Jimmer 144.58, Verde 105.08

Verde fell to **1–6 despite scoring 819.60 points**, the third-highest total in the league. This week, however, the victim narrative took a hit: he started a 2.40-point player while leaving a 20-point performance on the bench, with another 13.90-point option unused.

Neither substitution alone would have erased the 39.50-point deficit, but it was impressive to have that much scoring talent and still assemble a lineup that looked like it had been selected by a malfunctioning random-number generator.

## The Roofless House Becomes a Condemned Property

### Roop 118.82, Holden 77.02

Holden had lost six straight after spending 728 FAAB and trading away enough players to qualify as a player-trafficking investigation.

The latest innovation was dropping and re-adding the same players repeatedly while continuing to lose football games.

The doorbell was gone, the roof had been sold twice, and the city had posted a demolition notice on the front lawn.

## The Battle of the Bottom-Feeders

### Aron 100.54, Gav 65.72

Gav entered the week having spent 810 FAAB and responded by putting up **65.72 points**, including a defence that scored minus four.

Aron won comfortably with 100.54, which was approximately the fantasy equivalent of beating a traffic cone in a footrace.

## The Most Expensive Mediocrity Money Can Buy

### Alastair 127.08, Jaynaan 119.30

This was one of the week’s better matchups, with Alastair getting 28.88 from his quarterback and a pair of 21-point performances.

Jaynaan left a **10-point defence on the bench** while starting Houston for three, and also had a zero-point starter. The defence swap alone would not have flipped the result, but it would have made this an extremely uncomfortable 0.78-point loss.

## The FAAB Arms Race

### Rob 95.76, Mark 83.72

Rob had now won four straight despite producing another sub-100-point performance.

Mark spent **86 FAAB on Cleveland’s defence** and received five points for his trouble. That is 17.20 FAAB per point, which is impressive efficiency if you are running a charity for mediocre defensive units.

## Week 7 Awards

- **Biggest Ass-Kicking — Jimmer:** a 39.50-point demolition of Verde.
- **Worst Start/Sit Decision — Verde:** left 20 points on the bench while starting a 2.40-point dud. It was not enough to win, but certainly enough to make the defeat more embarrassing.
- **Most Condemned Franchise — Holden:** six straight losses, 728 FAAB spent and 77.02 points this week.
- **Most Expensive Five Points — Mark:** paid 86 FAAB for Cleveland’s defence.
- **Luckiest Winner — Rob:** secured another victory without cracking 100.
- **Biggest Collapse — Griff:** three consecutive losses after a 4–1 start.
- **Biggest Statement — Tom:** improved to 7–0 while comfortably leading the league in scoring.
- **Most Unnecessary Transaction Activity — Holden:** constantly added, dropped and reacquired players while the win column remained untouched.
- **Most Cursed Franchise — Verde:** third in scoring, 1–6, and now getting blown out even when scoring over 100.

---

Tom had spent 965 FAAB and gone 7–0. Holden had spent 728 and gone 1–6.

One was buying championships. The other was financing a demolition.
